OUR MISSION & VISION

What JOLA is here to do.

01 OUR VISION

A culture of intentional love.

To become a trusted Christian care-gifting brand that inspires a culture of intentional love—where people feel seen, remembered and celebrated through every season of life.

We envision JOLA becoming more than a gifting brand. We want to build a community that encourages people to notice one another, show up for one another and express God's love through meaningful acts of care.

02 OUR MISSION

Create gifts that communicate care.

To thoughtfully create faith-rooted gifting experiences that help people express what words alone sometimes cannot.

Through intentional curation, personal messages and meaningful details, we create gifts that bring comfort in difficult seasons, encouragement when strength is needed, and joy in moments worth celebrating.

Every JOLA is created with one purpose: To make someone feel seen.

THE WOMAN BEHIND JOLA

Meet the woman behind JOLA.

ANJOLA ADEKUNLE

The woman behind JOLA.

I'm a Product Designer, educator, and the founder of JOLA Gifting.

I create digital products that are centred around people, and I'm passionate about helping young people discover their potential.

I studied Mathematics at the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta, but people have always remained at the centre of the things I do.

JOLA grew from that same heart and one simple belief: everyone deserves to feel seen.

My faith is deeply woven into JOLA. I want every gift to remind someone: You are seen. You are loved. You are remembered.

My hope is that every JOLA carries more than beautiful things. I want it to carry encouragement, joy, hope, and care.

JOLA is only at the beginning of its story, and I'm excited to see the people, families and communities it becomes part of.

With love, Anjola Adekunle Founder, JOLA Gifting
